# Musique

A personal, single-user, self-hosted music player for a collection of audio files the owner keeps on their own machine.

## Language

**Music folder**:
The folder of audio files the owner keeps, which the app reads but never changes.
_Avoid_: Music root, music directory, music dir

**Library**:
The tracks and playlists the app found by scanning the music folder.
_Avoid_: Collection, catalogue
